Training portal • Pyaterochka

1. About the project

I led a year-long redesign of X5's training portal — the platform where employees take courses, sign up for events, and track their own learning progress. The redesign improved key metrics both for rank-and-file employees and for the training managers responsible for staff development programs.

In parallel, I led a team of two designers refactoring the design system for the company's HR services — a separate track that kept patterns consistent across several internal products.
